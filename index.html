About Electric Vehicles (EVs)

Electric Vehicles (EVs) are vehicles that use electricity to move instead of relying only on petrol or diesel. They use an electric motor and, in most cases, a rechargeable battery to power the vehicle.

Electric vehicles are available in different forms, including electric cars, electric bikes, and electric scooters. They are becoming popular because they can reduce fuel costs, produce no exhaust emissions while driving, and provide a quieter driving experience.

In India, many companies such as Tata Motors, Mahindra, Ola Electric, TVS Motor, Bajaj, and Ather Energy offer electric vehicles. Charging facilities and battery technology continue to play an important role in their development.

How Do Electric Vehicles Work?

A rechargeable battery stores electrical energy. This energy powers an electric motor, which turns the wheels and moves the vehicle. The battery can be recharged using a suitable charging point.

Why Are EVs Important?

  • They reduce dependence on petrol and diesel.
  • They produce no exhaust emissions while driving.
  • They can have lower running costs.
  • They generally produce less driving noise.
  • They can support sustainable transportation.

Types of Electric Vehicles

Electric vehicles are available in different types for different travel needs.

Electric Cars

Electric cars use batteries and electric motors. They are suitable for family travel and daily commuting.

Electric Scooters

Electric scooters are useful for short-distance travel and daily journeys within cities.

Electric Bikes

Electric motorcycles run on battery power and are available for daily travel and performance riding.

Benefits of Electric Vehicles

  • They produce no exhaust emissions while driving.
  • They can have lower running costs in suitable conditions.
  • They reduce dependence on petrol and diesel.
  • Electric motors generally produce less noise.
  • They can support cleaner transportation.

Challenges of Electric Vehicles

  • Some electric vehicles have a high purchase price.
  • Charging stations may not be available everywhere.
  • Charging can take longer than refuelling.
  • Battery performance can decrease over time.

How Do Electric Vehicles Work?

An electric vehicle stores energy in its battery. The battery supplies electricity to an electric motor, which turns the wheels and moves the vehicle.
