## 🌱 About the Project

**EcoMove** is an informative website dedicated to electric vehicles (EVs) and environmentally friendly transportation. It introduces different types of electric vehicles, explains how they work, showcases popular EV models in India, and highlights their benefits and challenges.

The project combines a clean, green-themed interface with informative content to promote awareness of sustainable mobility.

## ✨ Features

- 🚗 **Electric Vehicle Categories** — Explore electric cars, bikes, and scooters.
- 🇮🇳 **Popular EVs in India** — Discover selected electric vehicle models.
- ⚡ **How EVs Work** — Understand the basic working principle of electric vehicles.
- 🌍 **Environmental Benefits** — Learn how EVs can help reduce exhaust emissions.
- 🔋 **Benefits & Challenges** — Explore the advantages and limitations of EV adoption.
- 🧭 **Simple Navigation** — Move between sections with ease.
- 🎨 **Green-Themed UI** — Clean layout with a sustainability-inspired visual style.
- 💻 **CSS Animations** — Add subtle motion and visual interest to the interface.
